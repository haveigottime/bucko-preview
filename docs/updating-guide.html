Updating bucko.uk

The whole manual. Five minutes, once.

The site has its own editor at bucko.uk/admin. It looks like a normal form: gigs, tunes, videos, bio, links. Change something, hit Publish, and the site updates itself within a minute or two. No software, no uploading, nothing to break.

The whole job, every time

  1. Go to bucko.uk/admin and log in with the site password.
  2. Pick the section (Gigs, Music & videos, About & press, Links, Settings).
  3. Change what you need. Click Publish → Publish now.
  4. Wait a minute. Refresh bucko.uk.

One-time setup

  1. Bookmark bucko.uk/admin.
  2. Get the site password from Rory. That's it — no accounts to make.

First visit: click Login, type the password in the little window that pops up. After that it remembers you on that device.

The jobs you'll actually do

Add a gig

Gigs → Add gigs +. Fill in the date (there's a picker), venue, city, time, and the ticket link. Publish. Order doesn't matter — the site sorts by date and hides gigs automatically the day after they happen.

Sold out? Open the gig, flick Sold out on. It gets a stamp.
Free entry? Leave the ticket link blank and it says so.
Remove a gig? The × in its corner. (Past gigs go by themselves, you don't need to.)

Add a single or EP

Music & videos → Add releases +. It goes in at the top (newest first). The only fiddly bit is the Spotify box: open the release in Spotify, hit Share → Copy link, and paste in just the bit after open.spotify.com/ — ignore everything from ? onwards.

https://open.spotify.com/album/5byAofE0XSGLIUZEU8VSV2?si=yxz1Qq...

type in the box:   album/5byAofE0XSGLIUZEU8VSV2

The box will tell you if it doesn't look right. Apple Music link is optional — paste the whole thing.

Add a video

Same page, Add videos +. The YouTube ID is the 11 characters after youtu.be/ or watch?v=.

https://youtu.be/1jp16cV-z8E        →   type in the box:   1jp16cV-z8E

The first video in the list is the one that shows on the press page — drag the handle to reorder.

Bio, quotes, taglines, FFO

About & press. Plain boxes. Each bio paragraph is its own item. Press quotes have a Quote box and a Who box.

Press photos & downloads

About & press → Press downloads → Add +, give it a label like "Press photo 2027", click Choose a file, upload. Done — it's on the press page as a download.

WhatsApp, socials, shop link

Links. Paste the WhatsApp broadcast invite into its box and the button appears on the contact page by itself.

Switch the shop on

Settings → flick Shop is live on when the Shopify store is ready. Every Shop link on the site then goes straight to shop.bucko.uk instead of the Coming Soon page.

Get contact-form messages straight to your inbox

Right now the form opens the visitor's email app with the message pre-filled — works, but clunky. Go to web3forms.com, type in booking@bucko.uk, and they email you an "Access Key" (free, no account). Paste it into Settings → Contact form key.

If something looks wrong

Every publish is saved as a version, so nothing is ever lost. Message Rory with what you changed and he can put it back in about a minute. You can't delete the site from the editor, so click around freely.

Under the bonnet (you can ignore this)

The editor saves your changes as small text files in a code repository Rory looks after. Cloudflare notices and republishes bucko.uk. If the editor is ever down, Rory can make the same changes directly.

Stuck? Message Rory. Nothing you do in content.js is permanent.
